## What to build
Every pop-up notification arrives as a LAUNCH and leaves as a LAUNCH:
1. Kinds (map from `sev` and, if the alert text/key allows, from the type: wins, sales, milestones, rank-ups, quota hits = celebratory):
   - FIREWORK for success/celebration (`ok`, wins/sales/milestones): a shell with a spark trail rises from the bottom of the screen to the card's spot, bursts into a gold + crimson starburst (radial sparks with gravity and fading, a few glitter embers), and the card ignites out of the burst (scale/blur-in with a flash ring).
   - MISSILE for critical/warning (`crit`, `warn`): a sleek missile (inline SVG: dark body, crimson stripe, white nose, fins, flame) streaks across from the screen edge on a slightly curved path with a smoke/exhaust trail, locks onto the card's spot, impact shockwave ring + small spark spray, card slams in with a short red pulse (2 cycles, subtle).
   - ROCKET for info/neutral (`info`, chat/mention): a classic rocket (inline SVG, crimson band, gold flame) lifts off from below, flies up to the spot, the exhaust fades and the card deploys/docks (slide + settle), with a faint idle thruster flicker along the card's left edge until dismissed.
2. Leaving: when a card is dismissed (click, X, auto-timeout after 6 s) it fires its own thrusters (small flame under the card, 120 ms shake), then LAUNCHES upward off the screen leaving a smoke wisp and a star twinkle; clicking the card still navigates (`alGo`) but the card then launches away instead of vanishing. Auto-timeout leaving is subtler/faster than click-dismiss. Stack reflow: the remaining cards slide smoothly (no jumps).
3. Rendering approach: one fixed full-viewport `<canvas id=nfc>` overlay (pointer-events:none, z-index above pop-ups but below modals) for particles and trails, DOM/SVG for the rocket/missile bodies and the cards (CSS transforms/opacity only), pooled particles (<= 250), `requestAnimationFrame` loop runs ONLY while effects are active (zero idle cost, nothing runs when `document.hidden`). DPR-aware (cap 2). Keep `#pop` markup/semantics (role=status, aria-live) and readable text at the end state; the effect must never delay the information by more than ~700 ms (arrival timeline <= 900 ms total).
4. Sound (optional, tiny): if `D.snd` is on (existing `beep`), add a short WebAudio whoosh/pop synthesized (no files) layered with the existing beep; respect autoplay (silent if the context is suspended).
5. Phone (<= 560 px): scaled-down trajectories, cards full-width at the top as today, tap targets unchanged; effect canvas capped for performance. Wide screens with the rail open: aim at the shifted position.
6. Accessibility + safety: `prefers-reduced-motion` / the app's `RM` flag / `.redmo` class -> no flying objects or particles, just a 150 ms fade/slide (keep a subtle colour flash). A kill switch `localStorage.owq_nf_off='1'` restores the original pop-ups exactly. Errors in the effect code must never block a notification (wrap in try/catch; always render the card).
7. Provide a demo/test hook `nfDemo(kind)` (kinds: ok, crit, warn, info, burst of 5) and, if Settings already has a "test alert/notification" button, make it use real `pushAlert`; do not add new settings UI unless trivial.
8. Performance: 10 alerts in 2 seconds must stay smooth (cap concurrent flyers at 3, queue the rest with 250 ms stagger, collapse to the plain card beyond 6 queued); no layout thrash; no memory growth after 200 alerts (check listeners/DOM nodes).
