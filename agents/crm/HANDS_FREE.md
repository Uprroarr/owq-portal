# What the new Clients CRM does for you (plain language)

## Happens automatically (no taps)
- Every new lead gets a "first call" task the second it is added (quick capture, import, the + New lead form or an inbound lead), so it sits at the
  top of Today with a "New lead 3m" timer until someone calls.
- Today sorts everybody for you: appointments today, brand-new leads (fastest first), overdue follow-ups, then the rest of today. Each row says why it is there.
- Calling hours: the CRM knows the client's state and shows their local time; people outside 8 AM - 9 PM their time drop down the list.
- After a call outcome the next step is scheduled for you: no answer or voicemail -> another attempt tomorrow at 10 AM; interested -> follow-up in 2 days;
  appointment -> the appointment is booked and the stage moves; not interested or do not contact -> follow-ups stop.
- Marking a task Done schedules a sensible next touch when nothing else is planned for an active lead (1 day for new, 2 for contacted, 3 for quoted, 7 for underwriting).
- Logging a policy for a client links it to their record and moves the stage: Submitted -> Applied, Issued -> Issued/Delivery plus a "deliver the policy and ask for
  referrals" task, Paid -> Client, Declined -> back to Quoted plus a "recover with another carrier" task. The record shows how many months of advance are still at risk.
- Alerts: a new lead waiting more than 5 minutes, an appointment in the next 30 minutes, and follow-ups due all ping the alert rail (once each).
- Duplicates are caught when you add or import someone with the same phone, email, or name + age.
- Everything is shared live with the team (or saved on the device when the shared database is not available). Agents see their own clients plus unassigned leads; the owner sees everyone.

## One tap
- Call (opens the phone dialer), Text (opens your messages app with the right words already written: intro, missed-call, appointment confirmation or delivery), Email.
- Outcome after a call: No answer, Voicemail, Interested, Appointment set, Call back, Not interested, Wrong number, Do not contact.
- Done / Snooze (in 1 hour, tomorrow 10 AM, Monday ...), move a deal on the Pipeline (drag on a computer, pick the stage on a phone), claim an unassigned lead.
- Undo right after any change.
- Paste a lead in one line ("Mary Jones 412-555-0102 62 PA final expense facebook") and press Enter; paste a spreadsheet or drop a CSV to import a list.

## Not possible from the portal alone (needs an outside service and an owner decision)
- Sending texts or emails by itself, at night or while nobody has the page open. A web page cannot send SMS/email: the CRM prepares the message and opens your
  own phone or mail app. Fully automatic sending needs a provider such as Twilio, GoHighLevel or an email service connected to the portal.
- Leads flowing in by themselves from Facebook or a lead vendor (needs a webhook/integration). Today they are pasted or imported in one step.
- Automatic license-state checks and carrier-specific chargeback rules.
